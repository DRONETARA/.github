## Bringing stories to the sky

Dronetara is an India-based drone light show company combining creative storytelling with precision flight technology. We create custom aerial experiences for weddings, brand launches, corporate events, festivals, and public celebrations.

Our engineering work connects the whole show workflow: designing formations, previewing choreography, coordinating fleets, and developing the hardware that brings light to the sky.

## What we're building

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Show design** | Blender-based tools for formations, animation, lighting, and choreography. |
| **Visualization** | Previewing drone shows and reviewing motion before field operations. |
| **Fleet operations** | Ground control interfaces and server infrastructure for coordinating drones. |
| **Hardware & firmware** | Research and prototype development for lightweight show drones, onboard lighting, positioning, and communications. |

Hardware research includes **Halo-249**, a preliminary lightweight show drone concept integrating flight control, positioning, communications, and LEDs. It remains in engineering development.
